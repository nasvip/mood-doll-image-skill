# 20 种情绪表情器

情绪只驱动眼、眉、嘴、下颌和肩颈。不改脸型、发际、鼻梁、配饰。半睁眼 + 视线偏离镜头，是这套系统的母表情。

用户给中文名或编号即可。写入 prompt 时用对应英文段，不要堆中文形容词。

## 母表情（未指定时使用 01）

半睁的上眼睑压住瞳孔上缘，虹膜偏向画面一侧，嘴微启或平抿，下颌放松，像在看一件不值得解释的事。

## 表情库

01 厌世冷眼 — half-lidded eyes glancing sideways, bored mouth slightly parted, chin in hand optional, “I see it, I will not engage.”

02 不可一世 — chin slightly lifted, eyes looking down the nose, one brow calmly high, mouth closed in a faint smirk.

03 嚣张挑衅 — head tilted, eyes locked then sliding off-axis, lips parted as if daring someone to speak.

04 轻蔑嫌弃 — one-sided smirk, eyes narrowed, nose faintly scrunched, gaze sliding away from the subject.

05 搞怪斜眼 — extreme side-eye, pupils parked in the corner, deadpan mouth, comic stillness.

06 疯感轻笑 — small unhinged smile, eyes bright but unfocused, as if laughing at a private joke.

07 狂气失控 — wider eyes, messy hair energy, grin showing a hint of teeth, shoulders more open.

08 无语凝视 — fully frontal stare, lids heavy, mouth a flat line, long silent beat.

09 高冷审判 — symmetrical face, eyes level and cold, mouth closed, posture upright, evaluating.

10 坏笑恶作剧 — asymmetric grin, eyes crescent but not kind, like planning a prank.

11 疯批气质 — glamorous chaos, smudged energy without actual smeared makeup unless asked, ecstatic vacancy.

12 摆烂到底 — slouched neck, cheek crushed against palm or desk, eyes almost shut, zero ambition.

13 拽王模式 — lean back, one hand near face or collar, eyes half closed, fashion-arrogant stillness.

14 阴阳怪气 — polite smile that does not reach the eyes, head slightly tilted, venom under courtesy.

15 黑化前夜 — darker catchlight, smile gone, gaze steady and too quiet, before the outburst.

16 慵懒上头 — warm sleepy lids, soft mouth, body melted into chair or sofa, pleasure without energy.

17 目中无人 — looking past the camera, as if the viewer is furniture.

18 戏精附体 — theatrical three-quarter pose, over-articulated hand, magazine acting.

19 反骨模式 — jaw set, eyes sharp from under bangs or glasses, refusal in the neck angle.

20 宇宙级无所谓 — maximum flat affect, eyes elsewhere, shoulders loose, the universe can wait.

## 叠加规则

一次只用一种主情绪。若用户要「厌世但在笑」，以 01 为眼，以 06 或 10 为嘴，并在 prompt 里写清「eyes from mood 01, mouth from mood 06」。

手部可以服务表情（托腮、扶镜、捏杯、抵唇），但不要让手挡住双眼。
