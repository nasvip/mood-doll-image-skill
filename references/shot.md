# 镜头与构图库

镜头决定故事感。同一张脸，超特写是情绪标本，鱼眼是失控，远景是世界观。

## 常用机位

- 脸部超特写 FACE_MACRO — crop from forehead to chin or even one eye and cheek. Catchlights sharp. Pores become resin grain, not documentary skin.
- 眼部超特写 EYE_MACRO — one eye fills the frame, heavy lid, freckles, wet highlight.
- 半身三点 MEDIUM — chest-up, classic editorial, hand can enter frame.
- 全身 FULL — head-to-shoe, environment readable, doll proportions must be stated.
- 仰拍 LOW — camera at waist or knee, subject towers, jawline emphasized. Good for 不可一世 / 拽王.
- 俯拍 HIGH — camera above brow, subject looks smaller or more exhausted. Good for 摆烂 / 慵懒.
- 鱼眼 FISHEYE — strong barrel distortion, foreground hand or cup huge, background bends. Good for 狂气 / 戏精.
- 怼脸 PUSH — 24–35mm look, face close, ears and nose slightly large, intimate and rude.
- 电影远景 WIDE — tiny figure in architecture or city, face still readable if DOLL_2+.
- 过肩 / 双人 TWO_SHOT — two identities, clear left/right, shared desk or shared cup.

## 写入句式

```
Camera: [shot name], [focal-length feel], [angle], [what is in focus], [what is cropped].
```

示例：

```
Camera: medium editorial portrait, 85mm feel, slight low angle, sharp eyes, creamy background bokeh, hand supporting the cheek.
```

## 选择建议

- 要表情成立 → FACE_MACRO 或 MEDIUM
- 要故事 → MEDIUM + 道具（杯子、电脑、扇、杂志）
- 要世界观 → WIDE 或巨物
- 要海报冲击 → FISHEYE 或 LOW + 满版服装色块
